CONTROL · LEARNING · DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS

Learning, understanding, and controlling complex dynamical systems.

I develop theory and algorithms for reliable intervention in dynamical systems — from partially observed and heterogeneous control problems to learned models, neural representations, and physical systems.

FOUNDATIONS

Learning, Generalization & Robustness in Control

When can controllers be learned efficiently, generalized across systems, and remain reliable under uncertainty or adversarial corruption?

Policy gradient · LQG

On the Gradient Domination of the LQG Problem

History-based controller parameterizations reveal a benign optimization landscape for partially observed LQG and enable global convergence guarantees.

Multitask control

Multitask LQG Control: Performance and Generalization Bounds

A common lifted controller across heterogeneous tasks, with performance and generalization characterized through task similarity.

Adaptive · Robust

Adversarially Robust Multitask Adaptive Linear Quadratic Control

Multitask adaptive control under system heterogeneity and corrupted model updates, combining identification, clustering, and resilient aggregation.

02

INFERENCE-TIME CONTROL

Control of Learned Dynamics

How can pretrained models be treated as dynamical systems and reliably steered at inference time without retraining them?

Generative dynamics

CONIC-FLOW

Cast constrained flow-matching inference as optimal control and use receding-horizon primal–dual optimization to trade trajectory deviation against reward while enforcing terminal constraints.

pretrained flow→RH control→feasible sample
Representation dynamics

Robust Steerability of Language Models

Model transformer depth as a finite dynamical process and use robust feedback control to characterize and improve steering reliability under distribution shift.

representations→robust feedback→reliable steering

Geometry & Control of Neural Dynamics

What low-dimensional structure organizes neural dynamics, and how can that structure be used to understand or shape neural computation?

Manifold geometry

NeuralFieldManifold

Use lag embeddings and topological structure to connect oscillatory field potentials with low-dimensional toroidal geometry.

LFP→lag embedding→latent topology
Neural sampling

Accelerated Inference in Continuous Attractor Networks

Study feedback mechanisms that accelerate sampling and concurrent model-evidence computation in structured neural state spaces.

attractor→feedback→faster inference
