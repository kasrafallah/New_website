CONTROL · LEARNING · DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS

Learning, understanding, and controlling complex dynamical systems.

I develop theory and algorithms for reliable intervention in dynamical systems — from partially observed and heterogeneous control problems to learned models, neural representations, and physical systems.

FOUNDATIONS

Learning, Generalization & Robustness in Control

When can controllers be learned efficiently, generalized across systems, and remain reliable under uncertainty or adversarial corruption?

[1]
Policy gradient · LQG

On the Gradient Domination of the LQG Problem

Kasra Fallah, Leonardo F. Toso, James Anderson

arXivPreprint · 2025

Establishes gradient domination for partially observed LQG through a finite-history input/output controller parameterization, with model-based and model-free convergence guarantees.

arXiv ↗
[2]
Multitask control

Multitask LQG Control: Performance and Generalization Bounds

Leonardo F. Toso*, Kasra Fallah*, Charis Stamouli*, George J. Pappas, James Anderson

CDCIEEE Conference on Decision and Control · 2026

Learns a shared lifted controller across heterogeneous LQG tasks and relates transfer performance and generalization to task similarity.

arXiv ↗
[3]
Adaptive · Robust

Adversarially Robust Multitask Adaptive Linear Quadratic Control

Kasra Fallah*, Leonardo F. Toso*, James Anderson

L4DCLearning for Dynamics & Control · 2026

Combines local identification, clustering, resilient aggregation, and certainty-equivalent control to obtain multitask adaptive guarantees under heterogeneity and adversarial corruption.

Proceedings ↗
02

INFERENCE-TIME CONTROL

Steering Large Learned Systems

How can pretrained generative and representation dynamics be reliably steered at inference time under constraints, uncertainty, and distribution shift?

[4]
Generative models · Optimal control

CONIC-FLOW: Constrained Inference-Time Optimal Control for Flow Matching Models

Shervin Khalafi*, Kasra Fallah*, Sung Cho, Dongsheng Ding, James Anderson, Alejandro Ribeiro

ICLRUnder review · 2027

Casts constrained flow-matching inference as an optimal-control problem and develops receding-horizon primal–dual methods with finite-horizon guarantees.

[5]
Language models · Robust control

Mind Control of LLMs via Closed-Loop Stimulation: Out-of-Distribution Robustness

Kasra Fallah*, Rudramani Singha*, Hannah Ghanei, Paul Sajda, James Anderson, Erfan Zabeh

ICLRUnder review · 2027

Models transformer representations as a finite dynamical system and develops a robust-control notion of steerability for reliable interventions under distribution shift.

03

GEOMETRY · NEURAL COMPUTATION

Geometry & Control of Neural Dynamics

What low-dimensional structure organizes neural dynamics, and how can that structure be used to understand or shape neural computation?

[6]
Neural geometry · Topology

NeuralFieldManifold: Reconstruction of LFP Manifolds with Lag Embedding

Kasra Fallah*, Haoyu Novak Chen, Rudramani Singha, Eunji Kong, Gergely Turi, Attila Losonczy, Erfan Zabeh*

NeurIPS2026

Connects oscillatory neural field dynamics to low-dimensional manifold geometry using lag embeddings, autoregressive structure, and topological signatures.

[5]
NeuroAI · Robust control · Cross-listed

Mind Control of LLMs via Closed-Loop Stimulation: Out-of-Distribution Robustness

Kasra Fallah*, Rudramani Singha*, Hannah Ghanei, Paul Sajda, James Anderson, Erfan Zabeh

ICLRUnder review · 2027

Transfers closed-loop and robust-control principles from neural stimulation to language-model representation dynamics, linking biological and artificial neural intervention under distribution shift.

04

PHYSICAL SYSTEMS

Optimization & Predictive Control of Physical Systems

How can physical structure be exploited to make nonlinear optimization and predictive control tractable and efficient?

[7]
Electric machines · Optimization

On the Unification of Optimal Current Reference Theory for Wound Rotor Synchronous Machines

Maxfield Parson-Scherban, Kasra Fallah, Navid Rahbariasr, Bernard Steyaert, James Anderson, Matthias Preindl

CDCIEEE Conference on Decision and Control · 2026

Unifies optimal current-reference design across operating regimes for wound-rotor synchronous machines through a structure-aware optimization framework.

arXiv ↗
[8]
Model predictive control · Electric machines

Applying Linear Parameter Varying Model Predictive Current Control to an Enhanced WRSM Model

Maxfield Parson-Scherban, Kasra Fallah, Navid Rahbariasr, Peng Wang, Bernard Steyaert, James Anderson, Matthias Preindl

SAESIEEE/CCAE Small and Smart Electrical Systems · 2026

Embeds an enhanced WRSM model in linear-parameter-varying model predictive current control to handle nonlinear machine behavior and operating constraints.
